Community, Craft,
& Collaboration

Whether you are looking to showcase your handmade goods on our retail shelves or host a pop-up market in our gallery space, we provide a physical home for independent makers to share their work with Anoka.

In-Store Retail & Consignment

We partner with regional makers, artists, and designers to feature small-batch goods on our shelves. From hand-thrown ceramics and stationery to pins and prints, we provide a curated physical retail space for your work to meet our community every day.

  • Supporting Local Makers

    We believe a neighborhood coffee shop should be a platform for regional creativity. By dedicating shelf space to independent Anoka artists, we help connect our community directly with the people who craft the goods they love.

  • Curated Goods We Welcome

    We partner with creators producing small-batch, tactile items. Our shelves highlight hand-thrown ceramics, fine art prints, stationery, custom enamel pins, stickers, and unique lifestyle accessories.

  • How Consignment Works

    We keep things transparent with a supportive retail consignment split. Makers benefit from steady daily foot traffic and rotating seasonal display terms, while our team handles daily merchandising and customer checkouts.

Host Your
Pop-Up or Workshop

Bring your craft, market, or creative class to our community hub. We offer flexible gallery booths, studio nooks, power access, and built-in local co-marketing to support independent makers and event hosts.

What

Whether you want to host a painting class, poetry slam or D&D night the coffee shop is available for rental.

When

The shop is available for rental most days after 5pm or prior to 5pm with a buy out price.

Price

Rental starts at $60 per hour depending on the complexity of your event and rental ask.
